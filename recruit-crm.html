Recruit CRM Company Page Redesign

To redesign the Company Page in Recruit CRM to improve usability, reduce visual clutter, and enable recruiters to manage jobs, tasks, and candidates with maximum efficiency and clarity.

My Role Lead Product Designer
Discipline B2B UX & Workflow Hierarchy
Platform Web SaaS Platform
Format 15-Slide Interactive Presentation

Presentation Deck (15 Slides)

Navigate with ← → arrow keys or click the side buttons
Slide 1 of 15

Workspace UI: Before vs. After

Toggle between the original interface and the redesign to see how cognitive load was drastically reduced.

New Redesign
01

Top Bar Hierarchy Reorganized

Promoted core recruiter workflows by de-prioritizing generic marketing buttons ("Book a Demo"), styling "Upgrade to Premium" as a subtle gold crown icon, and making "+ Add New" globally prominent.

02

Actionable Empty States

Replaced passive "No activities associated with this record yet!" with a delightful visual and a direct, guided "Add Task" primary button so recruiters immediately know their next step.

03

Collapsible About Company Section

Tucked dozens of secondary and often-empty company fields into an accordion that stays neatly collapsed by default, saving massive vertical screen real-estate for active jobs and candidate pipelines.

04

Action Button Grouping & Positioning

Moved "Add Job" and "View Assigned Candidates" down adjacent to the jobs table for clear contextual focus, and simplified verbose filter labels to clean, concise UI elements.
