Mobile App Case Study

Optimizing AOV for Zepto Quick-Commerce

My Role Lead Product Designer
Timeline 8 Weeks (Q3 2024)
Tools Figma, Miro, ProtoPie
Platforms iOS & Android Apps

01. Overview

Zepto is one of India's fastest-growing quick-commerce platforms delivering groceries in 10 minutes. While active users and order frequency grew rapidly, the business faced margin compression from small basket sizes. This project focused on redesigning checkout, cart recommendations, and pre-purchase bundles to increase Average Order Value (AOV) without hurting conversion rates or delivery velocity.

02. The Problem

Users were ordering single high-demand items (e.g. milk, bananas) under delivery subsidies. Cross-sell banners on the cart page felt intrusive and triggered choice paralysis, causing an 11% cart abandonment rate. We needed frictionless contextual add-ons and intelligent basket builders.

03. Design Process & Wireframes

We mapped out customer purchase friction points through session recordings and user interviews. By categorizing complementary impulse buys (breakfast essentials, late-night snacks, household staples), we introduced 1-tap bundle additions and progress milestones toward free delivery perks.

05. Business Outcome

The redesigned funnel was rolled out across 12 tier-1 cities with significant lifts in basket economics:

+18% Increase in Average Order Value (AOV)
3.2x Adoption of 1-tap impulse bundles
-9% Reduction in cart exit bounce rate
